About Michael

Helping Authors Find the Readers Their Books Were Written For.

My work in book marketing began with a straightforward question. Why do so many thoughtfully written books struggle to reach the readers who would love them? Over the past eight years, I have built my practice around answering that question for the authors I work with.

I have had the privilege of partnering with more than one hundred and fifty authors across fiction and nonfiction, promoting over four hundred books and helping reach more than five hundred committed readers through considered, well-planned campaigns. Twenty of the authors I have worked with have seen their books achieve bestseller status during our time together, a result that always reflects the quality of the writing as much as the marketing.

What I offer is not a quick formula. It is a careful, sustained approach built on genre knowledge, honest communication, and a deep respect for the relationship between an author and their readers.

What I Stand For

The Principles Behind Every Campaign.

These values are not slogans. They are the standards that shape how I plan campaigns, speak with authors, and spend their marketing budgets.

i.

Honesty Over Hype

Marketing should set realistic expectations. I will always tell you what is likely, what is possible, and what is simply out of anyone's control.

ii.

Craft Deserves Craft

Your writing was shaped with care. The marketing around it should be shaped with the same attention, not pieced together from templates.

iii.

Readers First, Always

Every campaign aims to respect readers, not interrupt them. Reaching the right people in the right way builds trust that lasts beyond one book.

iv.

Strategy Before Tactics

Tactics without strategy waste money and attention. Every activity I recommend connects to a clear goal and a clear reason.

v.

Long-Term Thinking

A single book is rarely the end of the story. I plan with your long-term author career in mind, not only the launch window.

vi.

Clear Communication

You will always know what is happening, what is working, and what we are adjusting. No vague updates and no buried reports.

My Approach

How I Work With Authors.

Every project begins with understanding, not assumptions. My process is designed to give your book the attention it deserves while making the work feel manageable on your side.

01

Listen Before Planning

I begin by learning about your book, your goals, your audience, and your publishing history. The strategy that follows is shaped by what I hear, not by a template applied in advance.

02

Research the Market

I study your genre, your readers, and your closest comparable titles. Decisions about positioning, pricing, and promotion all rest on that foundation.

03

Design a Clear Strategy

Together we agree on priorities, timelines, and what success realistically looks like. You see the full plan before any activity begins.

04

Execute With Care

Every creative asset, outreach message, and advertising campaign is handled with attention to quality, not speed alone. Your book deserves careful hands.

05

Measure and Refine

Campaigns are reviewed regularly, with adjustments made based on real performance data. Nothing is left running on autopilot once a plan is launched.

06

Plan for What Comes Next

Once a campaign ends, we look at what was learned and how it can shape future books, future launches, and the long-term growth of your author brand.
