Honest answers to the questions authors ask me most often, covering how I work, who I work with, and what you can realistically expect from professional book marketing.

A professional book marketer helps authors plan and carry out the activities that bring their books to the attention of relevant readers. That work can include positioning the book correctly, designing promotional materials, running advertising, reaching out to bloggers and reviewers, optimizing retailer listings, and shaping an ongoing marketing strategy.

In practical terms, my role is to think about your book the way a thoughtful publisher's marketing team would, then build a plan that fits your situation, your genre, and your resources.

If you are a first-time author, I will walk you through the process patiently and make sure you understand each stage before moving forward.

The underlying principles of good marketing stay consistent, but the specific tactics and messaging are always tailored to your category.

Yes. Amazon is central to most of the marketing work I do, because it is where a large share of book buying actually happens. Audience identification starts with careful reading. I look at your book, its themes, its tone, and its likely comparable titles. From there, I research the readers who buy and enjoy those comparable titles, the places where they discover new books, and the language they use when discussing what they love.

Advertising data, retailer categories, reader communities, and genre knowledge all feed into a working profile of your target reader. That profile then guides decisions about ad targeting, outreach, pricing, and messaging.

Marketing can significantly improve a book's chances, but it cannot guarantee outcomes.

No. Bestseller rankings depend on sales velocity, category competition, and timing, all of which fluctuate constantly. I do not use artificial or manipulative tactics to chase rankings. The goal is sustainable visibility and genuine reader interest, which tend to produce far better long-term results.
