A Note on Pricing

Tailored to the Book in Front of Me.

Every book, author, and goal is different. Rather than publishing fixed prices that may not reflect the realities of your specific project, I prefer to confirm pricing after a short conversation. That way, the quote you receive is grounded in the actual scope of work your book needs, not a generic estimate.

If a particular service is important to you or you have a budget in mind, mention it when you reach out. I will be straightforward about what is realistic and what I would recommend as the strongest use of your resources.

Beyond the Package Lists

What Every Package Quietly Includes.

Regardless of which package suits your project, every working relationship comes with a set of underlying standards that do not appear on any feature list.

You receive careful attention to the voice of your book, honest advice when something is not worth the investment, consistent communication throughout the engagement, and a steady focus on respecting both your budget and your readers.

These are not add-ons. They are the foundation of how I work, and they apply equally whether you choose the smallest or the largest package.
