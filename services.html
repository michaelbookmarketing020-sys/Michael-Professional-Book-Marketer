A Complete Range of Professional Book Marketing Services

From creative design and advertising to reader outreach, Amazon discoverability, publicity, and full marketing strategy. Every service is shaped around the voice of the author and the audience of the book.

01
Category One

Book Branding and Creative Design

A book's visual identity is often the first promise it makes to a reader. Strong branding and creative design communicate genre, tone, and professionalism before the first page is read. These services help authors present their work with the polish of a well-published title.

i.

Book Cover Design and Redesign

Covers crafted or refined to align with genre conventions and reader expectations, ensuring your book looks competitive on retailer shelves and category charts.

ii.

Book Mockup Design

Realistic 3D mockups and lifestyle presentations that make your book feel tangible in advertising, social graphics, and press materials.

iii.

Promotional Graphics

Quote cards, teaser visuals, and promotional imagery designed to carry your book's identity across every marketing channel.

iv.

Book Launch Graphics

A coordinated suite of visuals prepared for launch day announcements, countdowns, and release promotions.

v.

Author Branding

A consistent visual and verbal identity that gives you a recognizable presence across books, platforms, and campaigns.

vi.

Book Description Optimization

Sales descriptions rewritten to open with a strong hook, emphasise reader appeal, and improve conversion on retailer pages.

vii.

Author Biography Development

Professional author bios written in long and short formats for retailers, websites, interviews, and media use.

viii.

Amazon A+ Content Design

Rich visual modules for Amazon product pages that showcase your book, your brand, and your series in a polished way.

ix.

Book Trailer Creation

Short cinematic videos that capture the mood and premise of your book for use in advertising and social promotion.

x.

Short Promotional Videos

Compact video content tailored for ads, reels, and author updates, built to hold attention in a crowded feed.

Amazon Ads Management

ii.

Facebook and Instagram Advertising

iii.

Paid Promotional Campaigns

Coordinated promotional pushes through established book promotion channels, selected based on genre fit and audience quality.

iv.

Book Launch Promotion

A structured launch plan that combines advertising, outreach, and creative assets to give your release real momentum.

v.

Preorder Campaigns

Preorder strategy and promotion designed to build early interest and strengthen launch day performance.

vi.

Discount and Free Book Promotions

Strategic pricing promotions that attract new readers, generate reviews, and support broader marketing goals.

vii.

Backlist Promotion

Fresh marketing for previously published titles, giving older books renewed visibility and continued sales potential.

viii.

Book Series Promotion

Series-focused campaigns that encourage readers to move from book one through the rest of the series.

ix.

Audience Research

Careful identification of the readers most likely to connect with your book, including their reading habits and buying behaviour.

x.

Advertising Creative Development

Ad copy, imagery, and video creative written and designed to perform in competitive advertising environments.

Campaign Monitoring and Optimization

Ongoing review of performance data with regular adjustments to targeting, bids, and creative to improve results over time.

Book Blogger Outreach

Reader Community Outreach

Engagement with established reader groups and communities built around your book's genre and themes.

Book Club Promotion

Promotion to book clubs with discussion material and tailored outreach for reader-led selection.

v.

Newsletter Promotion

Placement in reader newsletters with established, engaged subscribers in your genre.

vi.

Email Marketing Campaigns

Email sequences, launch announcements, and ongoing reader communications written to feel personal rather than promotional.

vii.

Advance Reader Copy Campaigns

Managed ARC distribution to early readers who can help generate initial reviews and word of mouth ahead of launch.

viii.

Ethical Review Outreach

Review outreach that follows retailer and platform guidelines, focused on genuine reader feedback rather than shortcuts.

ix.

Reader Engagement Strategies

Ongoing strategies that keep readers interested between books, from updates to behind the scenes content.

Reader Retention Strategies

Retention plans that turn one-time readers into long-term followers of your writing and future releases.

Genre-Specific Audience Targeting

Targeting rooted in deep genre knowledge, so your book is placed in front of readers who already love what you write.

Amazon and Book Discoverability

Amazon remains the largest marketplace for books, and discoverability within it depends on the quality of your listing, keywords, categories, and metadata. These services focus on the technical foundations that make your book easier to find long after launch.

i.

Amazon KDP Listing Optimization

A full review and refinement of your KDP listing, including title fields, subtitle, series details, and supporting metadata.

ii.

Book Keyword Research

Careful research into the keywords and phrases readers actually use when looking for books like yours.

iii.

Amazon Category Research

Selection of relevant and competitive categories that give your book the best chance of visibility and chart placement.

iv.

Book Metadata Optimization

Refinement of all metadata elements, from contributor roles to series information, to support both readers and algorithms.

v.

Amazon Author Page Optimization

A polished Author Central page with a professional bio, complete bibliography, and relevant cross promotion of your titles.

vi.

Book Description Improvement

Formatted, keyword aware descriptions designed to engage readers and improve conversion on your product page.

vii.

Retailer Listing Optimization

Consistent, well-optimized listings across other major retailers to support wide distribution strategies.

viii.

Book Pricing Strategy

Pricing recommendations based on genre norms, book length, series position, and overall marketing goals.

ix.

Search Visibility Improvement

Strategies to strengthen your book's visibility in retailer search results over time, not only during launch.

x.

Goodreads Discovery Strategy

Goodreads profile setup, listing optimization, and planning for giveaways, shelves, and community visibility.

xi.

Book Discoverability Research

A broader review of where readers in your genre currently find new books and how your title can appear in those places.
